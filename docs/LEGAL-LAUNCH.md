## Clear acceptance entry across browser and agents

Browser `GatewayAccess` → `/auth/login` → OIDC callback, direct `/auth/login`, hosted MCP OAuth login and local CLI browser login share the same backend-rendered notice:

- Displays linked `Terms of Service` (`/terms`) and `Privacy Notice` (`/privacy`), eligibility confirmation (at least 18, legal capacity, organizational authority where applicable) when active, effective date (`Effective: ...` or `Not effective` in draft mode) and SHA-256 fingerprint (with zero public version strings), plus a `Continue to sign-in` POST action.
- Preserves signed PKCE/state/nonce transactions, callback verification and transaction-bound pending MCP return. Policy identifiers (`hosted-terms-2026-11-16` when published, `hosted-terms-draft-2026-11-16` when draft) and SHA-256 digests are chosen exclusively on the server; client-supplied versions, workspace invitation `accepted_at` and OAuth grants never substitute for human Terms assent.
- Keeps four decisions distinct: human Terms acceptance; privacy information (and any genuinely optional consent); agent scopes/workspace spending authority; and explicit workspace funding-owner enrollment/replacement, Checkout approval or opt-in automatic-recharge authorization. Continuing to sign in never creates a card mandate, enables automatic recharge, executes a customer DPA or waives mandatory withdrawal/payment rights.

## Focused acceptance checks

- Browser, direct login, MCP and local-agent entry reach the same clean notice; pending OAuth return is preserved.
- Published HTML and `.md` endpoints emit the exact approved copy and effective date without draft banners or public version numbers, `/refunds` and `/refunds.md` redirect cleanly to `/terms#credits-and-refunds` and `/terms.md`, and DPA and Subprocessors retain their unexecuted-template notice.
- Unconfigured/self-hosted deployments remain Draft (`mode: 'draft'`), accept no Terms on sign-in and write zero `mr_terms_receipts` rows.
- Cross-origin/forged initiation, stale policy identifiers, unreviewed Terms text edits, failed callbacks and cancelled login cannot mint an acceptance receipt or bypass OIDC checks.
- Never perform a paid generation or real payment as an automated UI test; qualify hosted identity and Stripe environments separately under bounded operator procedures.
