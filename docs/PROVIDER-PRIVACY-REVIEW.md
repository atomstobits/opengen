# Provider privacy and agreement review

Public mechanisms checked on 9 November 2026. This is an operator review index and record format, not executed agreements or a claim every catalog route has a covered processing chain. Complete actual account/customer arrangements privately before relying on a particular protection. Existing applicable agreements may already incorporate the relevant terms; this procedure does not instruct operators to sign seven fresh contracts.

Separate OpenGen's instructed-content processing from account, identity, payment, security and legal functions. Determine the actual role for each purpose rather than calling every vendor a subprocessor. Customer-selected assistants, webhooks and destinations have their own selecting parties and terms.

## Example: Wan Standard Singapore

Prioritize the actual enabled route, not every advertised catalog entry. For `alibaba/ap-southeast-1/wan-3.0`, match the actual operation and endpoint to the documented `wan3.0-video` model and the account's approved scope. A Standard live test does not establish Prime, another region or another supplier's contract coverage.

The [Model Studio retention policy](https://www.alibabacloud.com/help/en/model-studio/data-retention-policy) describes a general prompt/response default of no more than 30 days, with legal/security exceptions and separate treatment for stateful features. It excludes asynchronous task management from the stated ZDR coverage; a workspace ZDR switch does not establish Wan ZDR. That general policy does not separately prove the deletion period for every Wan video, reference object, log or backup.

The [Wan3 API](https://docs.modelstudio.console.alibabacloud.com/en/model-studio/wan3-video-generation-api-reference) documents asynchronous task/result availability, including 24-hour task-query and result-link limits. Availability expiry is separate from proof that every supplier copy was erased. Preserve those distinctions when recording the route's actual settings, supplier answers and agreements. OpenGen-owned private delivery has its own retention and access controls; see [Asset Privacy](ASSET-PRIVACY.md).

## Private agreement record

Use one small record per actual functional relationship, outside Git and public artifacts:

- Customer and recipient legal parties; applicable account/product/plan and agreement/DPA version/incorporation evidence reference. Do not include credentials, KYC documents or full billing exports.
- Covered purposes, information, processing role, exact model/operation/route and permitted fallbacks; actual onward recipients and appointing party.
- Inference/storage/support/access locations; relevant training settings; separately evidenced task, media, reference, log and backup lifecycle; available return/deletion route.
- Any required transfer determination and actually applicable completed instrument/assessment. A vendor's public SCC/DPF mechanism does not execute a customer-to-OpenGen arrangement.
- Actual advance notice and objection periods, subscribed receiving contact and agreed customer notification process. An objection window is not an advance-notice minimum.
- Reviewer/date, covered scope, exclusions, unresolved facts and next action. Keep authoritative public URLs separate from private evidence of account incorporation.

The proposed OpenGen DPA's thirty-day change rule remains the default. A different supplier-internal process must be identified and expressly authorized under its section 6, with actual information and required opportunity to object. A public supplier list update cannot silently change that promise or the authorized customer chain. Preserve purpose, confidentiality and responsibility throughout the onward chain.

## Reference mechanism

[OpenRouter's DPA](https://openrouter.ai/data-processing-agreement) and [fal's DPA](https://fal.ai/legal/data-processing-addendum) provide useful scoped processing/security/recipient schedules and change procedures. [Higgsfield business help](https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/business/how-do-i-submit-a-dmca-notice-or-legal-inquiry) routes DPA/security procurement through Enterprise. [treg Privacy](https://treg.to/privacy) distinguishes hosted and self-hosted responsibility. Use those mechanisms with the actual OpenGen chain; their contracts and pass-through/storage designs do not establish OpenGen's own account coverage.
