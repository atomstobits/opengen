## Pricing and billing boundaries

The [fal H3 Max Turbo text-to-video page](https://fal.ai/models/minimax/h3-max-turbo/text-to-video) currently describes a November 1–15, 2026 promotion: USD 0.0175/second at 480p, USD 0.028 at 768p and USD 0.056 at 1080p. Five seconds of text-only 480p output has a list estimate of **USD 0.0875**. Standard 480p pricing resumes November 16 at USD 0.025/second; the source does not state a cutoff timezone. This exact accelerated variant remains distinct from canonical MiniMax H3 Max.

fal's pricing response reports base unit prices. Its [cost estimator](https://fal.ai/docs/platform-apis/v1/models/pricing/estimate) accepts unit quantities or historical call quantities, rather than a complete resolution/input configuration. These APIs do not create a guaranteed provider charge ceiling. Do not apply an output-only text-generation estimate to reference inputs without qualifying their additional charges.

fal's [billing-events API](https://fal.ai/docs/platform-apis/v1/models/billing-events) supports exact request filtering and returns `cost_total` in USD plus the equivalent integer `cost_estimate_nano_usd`. It requires the BILLING/FULL permission presets, defaults to the last 24 hours, permits at most a 90-day date span and uses cursor pagination. An absent event or HTTP 403 is not evidence of a zero charge. The current key's billing restriction is a concrete reconciliation limitation.

Other reviewed public tariffs remain separate from account billing evidence: [MiniMax H3 pricing](https://platform.minimax.io/docs/pricing/overview), [xAI Imagine pricing](https://docs.x.ai/developers/pricing), [Alibaba Wan Standard](https://www.alibabacloud.com/help/en/model-studio/wan3-0-video) and [Wan Prime](https://www.alibabacloud.com/help/en/model-studio/wan3-0-video-prime). Token-priced and otherwise incomplete request formulas remain blocked for paid smoke testing until a bounded configuration is qualified. Provider discounts, taxes and eventual charges are not inferred from public list prices.
