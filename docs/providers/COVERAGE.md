## Remaining explicit gaps

- **Additional operations:** managed execution does not cover every operation advertised by each upstream model. Unsupported inputs fail preflight; they are not rewritten into different operations. In particular, mixed audio/video references, draft-to-final workflows, layer decomposition, and direct OpenAI image editing remain outside these adapters.
- **Grok legacy video edit/extend:** provider inherits source dimensions (capped at 720p) and edit duration. Managed quotes remain unpriced (`rates: []`); BYOK/unbounded execution enforces source-ratio / non-override constraints in preflight.
- **Qwen outside Singapore:** regional model availability needs qualification. In particular, fal's “Qwen Image 3” name alone does not establish equivalence to Alibaba's separately named `qwen-image-3.0-pro`. The published Qwen base weights do not establish an API offering for the base checkpoint.
- **Runway Seedance and BFL video-edit:** Runway Seedance and BFL `/v1/flux-tools/video-edit-v1` remain comparison-only (BFL `video-edit` is rejected in preflight, including BYOK); 19 routes across all 12/12 providers are `live-verified` for their exact dated hosted smoke configurations above (two November 6 Wan 3.0 routes, two November 19 OpenAI/Runway routes, nine November 27 official routes and six November 27 provider-coverage routes), while Alibaba HappyHorse 1.1 in `cn-beijing` and `eu-central-1` plus the remaining 159 routes and untested operations, resolutions and durations remain `documented`.
- **Regional verification:** public endpoint/model documentation does not establish actual access on a user's account, per-request inference location, or storage residency. US-hosted fal access remains account-enabled and comparison-only.
- **Live validation:** only the hosted Wan configurations (November 6, 2026), the two post-cutover smoke configurations (November 19, 2026), the nine hosted official-route smoke configurations (November 27, 2026), and the six hosted provider-coverage smoke configurations above (November 27, 2026) have dated hosted generation and private-delivery evidence (`19` of `178` routes, covering `12/12` providers; all five initial holds across Runway, BFL image routes, fal and Google have been audited and settled on `https://opengen.dev`, leaving `0` pending smoke holds). All other route/configuration coverage remains unverified/pending and requires bounded real account smoke tests. Fixtures do not establish generation quality, reliability or billing accuracy.

No adapter sends blind retries after an ambiguous paid submission. Upstream failure messages/bodies are redacted. Missing usage or tariffs leaves billing pending reconciliation. Generation completion is distinct from settlement and durable output retention.
