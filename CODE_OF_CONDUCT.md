# Community conduct

OpenGen welcomes people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Be respectful, constructive and specific. Discuss the work and its effects without personal attacks.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate privacy violations and publication of another person's confidential information are unacceptable. Do not post credentials or private generation inputs.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful material, close discussions, or restrict participation to protect the community. Report concerns privately to a repository maintainer; use the private security channel for vulnerabilities. Maintainers should explain decisions, protect reporter privacy and consider context consistently.
